Cookie and local-storage policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This site uses browser storage for preferences and, only with permission, Google Analytics cookies. It does not use advertising cookies.

Storage used without analytics

  • theme is local storage that remembers the light or dark display preference until it is changed or browser storage is cleared.
  • mdv-analytics-consent is local storage that records whether Analytics was accepted or declined. The site asks again after six months.

Local storage remains on your device and is not automatically sent with web requests like a cookie. These entries do not contain your name or email address.

Optional Google Analytics cookies

If you select Accept analytics, Google Analytics may set:

  • _ga, to distinguish visitors;
  • _ga_G-XQQDQDW68N, to maintain session state.

This site configures those cookies to expire after 90 days and prevents the Analytics tag from loading before acceptance. Google documents the standard cookie behaviour in GA4 cookie usage on websites.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use Change analytics choice below or Analytics settings in the footer. Withdrawing disables further Analytics requests from this site after the page reloads. It does not erase information already processed by Google. You can also delete this site’s cookies and local storage in your browser.

Necessary hosting and security logs are controlled by GitHub Pages rather than by this site’s cookie controls. See the privacy notice for details.
