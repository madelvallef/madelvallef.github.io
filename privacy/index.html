Privacy notice

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Who operates this site

This is the personal academic website of Miguel Del Valle, economist, based in Santiago, Chile. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to mdelvalle@fen.uchile.cl.

Data handled when you visit

  • Hosting and security. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation.
  • Optional analytics. Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you select Accept analytics. It may receive page views, approximate location, referral source, and browser or device information. This site does not send names, email addresses, a configured User-ID, or form contents to Analytics. Google explains its processing in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Your preferences. The site stores your theme and analytics choice locally in your browser. These preferences are not sent to the site owner.

The site has no account registration, checkout, newsletter, comment system, advertising, or web contact form. If you use an email link, your message is handled by your chosen email provider and the recipient’s email provider, not by this website. The address, content, and metadata of an email are used only to read and respond to the message and are kept only while needed for that exchange, an ongoing academic relationship, or a legitimate record-keeping or legal purpose. They are not added to a marketing list.

Purposes and choice

Hosting data is handled to deliver and secure the site. Analytics data is used only to understand aggregate readership and improve the site. Analytics is based on your choice: if you decline or make no choice, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded.

The site does not sell personal data and does not use Analytics for advertising or personalized advertising. Analytics advertising signals are disabled in the site’s configuration.

Providers, transfers, and retention

GitHub and Google are service providers headquartered outside Chile and may process data in other countries under their own terms and safeguards. Their exact processing locations, subprocessors, and transfer mechanisms are governed by their current service and data-processing terms; those terms require periodic review by the site owner. The Analytics property is configured for the shortest available user-level and event-level retention period (two months), without extending the period when a user returns; aggregated reports may be retained separately by Google. Analytics cookies on this site are configured to expire after 90 days. The local record of your analytics choice expires after six months, after which the site asks again.

Security and data minimisation

The site uses static hosting and has no application database or form endpoint. Third-party fonts are hosted locally, optional Analytics is blocked until acceptance, enhanced measurement and advertising signals are disabled, and only standard page-view measurement is enabled. Access to the hosting and Analytics accounts is controlled by those providers’ account-security mechanisms. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to request information about personal data, correction, deletion or cancellation, blocking, objection, or portability. To make a request concerning data controlled by this site owner, email mdelvalle@fen.uchile.cl. Requests relating to GitHub or Google processing may also need to be directed to those providers.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the site’s data practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
